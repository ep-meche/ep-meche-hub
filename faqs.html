Have a question about planning your Mechanical Engineering degree?

Browse the questions below for guidance on course planning, registration, focus areas, academic resources, and graduation. When a question involves an official policy or deadline, use the linked EP resource for the most current information.

Degree Planning & Focus Areas
Do I need to declare a Mechanical Engineering focus area?
Yes. Students should complete the Mechanical Engineering Focus Area Track Declaration when beginning to plan their coursework. Use the Focus Areas page to review the available focus areas and related courses.
Can I change my focus area later?
If your academic or professional interests change, review the focus-area requirements and update your plans accordingly. Use the Focus Area Track Declaration for the current declaration process or contact the program team through the Forms & Contacts page if you need guidance.
How do I know which courses count toward my focus area?
Start with the Focus Areas page and the official Mechanical Engineering Courses page. You can also review the Degree Requirements page when planning your program.
How many courses are required for the master's degree?
The Mechanical Engineering master's degree is structured around the program's published degree requirements. Review the current Degree Requirements page for the official requirements and current details.
Is 535.641 Mathematical Methods for Engineers required in the first semester?
535.641 Mathematical Methods for Engineers is commonly used in early degree planning, but students should follow their individual degree plan and any advising guidance that applies to their situation. Use the Degree Requirements page and your degree audit to confirm what is needed.
Courses & Registration
How do I register for courses?
Check the Academic Calendar for registration dates, then register through SIS.
Where can I see which courses are offered in an upcoming semester?
Use the Course Offering Schedule for planning and the official SIS schedule when registering. Course offerings can change, so use SIS for the current semester schedule.
Is the Course Offering Schedule guaranteed?
No. The Course Offering Schedule is intended as a planning resource. Course offerings may change, so students should verify the official schedule in SIS when planning registration for a specific semester.
What should I do if a course I want is full?
Check SIS for current enrollment information and any available registration options. If you still need assistance, use the Forms & Contacts page to determine the appropriate contact.
What if SIS will not let me register?
For enrollment or account-management issues, use Student Case Management. This is the appropriate starting point for many SIS, enrollment, and account-related questions.
Can I take courses outside of Mechanical Engineering?
Review the published Degree Requirements before planning courses outside the program. If you are unsure whether a course can be applied to your degree, contact the program team before registering.

Thesis, Independent Study & Special Planning
Can I complete a thesis as part of my master's degree?
Review the current Mechanical Engineering degree requirements and thesis guidance before making this decision. Use the program's advising resources for questions about eligibility, planning, and registration.
How do I get started with a thesis?
Begin by reviewing the program's thesis guidance and determining whether a thesis fits your academic goals and degree plan. Contact the appropriate faculty or program resource through Forms & Contacts for next steps.
What is an Independent Study?
An Independent Study is a faculty-supervised academic experience built around an approved topic or area of study. Students should work with the appropriate faculty member and confirm the registration and approval process before enrolling.
